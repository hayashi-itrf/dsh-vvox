**Speech synthesis supports Japanese only.** Replies are read without translation. Text in other languages may be skipped or pronounced as Japanese.

Choose a character, voice style, speed and volume in **Voice settings** below. Preview before saving. Saved settings apply to every chat.

Speech starts while DSH is writing. Reasoning, tools, code and history are excluded. Use `/voicevox stop` to stop automatic speech, or `/voicevox on` and `off` to toggle it. Audio plays in this DSH window. Enable speech here if prompted. FFmpeg is not required.

VOICEVOX versions without streaming synthesis automatically use standard synthesis one sentence at a time.

When using generated audio, credit the voice and follow the terms for VOICEVOX and the selected character. [VOICEVOX terms](https://voicevox.hiroshiba.jp/term/) · [Character list and terms](https://voicevox.hiroshiba.jp/)
